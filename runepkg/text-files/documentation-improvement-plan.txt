1. PURPOSE & CORE OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The purpose of this document is to establish a living roadmap for refining,
improving, and harmonizing all existing documentation within `runepkg/text-files/`,
`docs/`, and root Markdown files (`README.md`, `RUNAS.md`).

As runepkg has evolved into an absolute tooling rescue package manager driven by
the triad of `runepkg_psychic` (linker & symlink prophecy), `runepkg_antimatter`
(decontamination & boundary guard), and `arch-utils` (clean-room static C89 suite),
this plan ensures that all theoretical foundations, historical lore, and technical
specifications remain impeccably structured, accurate, and cross-referenced.


2. CORE REFINEMENT PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Single Source of Truth for Technical Specs:
   - Ensure primary architectural specs (`DESIGN.md`, `not_some_ordinary_plan.txt`,
     `not_full_static_plan.txt`) accurately reflect the 15 `*matter` C89 modules,
     the 14 `arch-utils` applets, and `musl-gcc -static` zero-dependency guarantees.

2) Preservation of Historical Lore & Theoretical Milestones:
   - Retain and polish historical archives (`matryoshka_reasoning.txt`, `psychic_theory.txt`,
     `ccc-archgate.txt`, `runas_design.txt`, `copilot_opinion.txt`) as an immutable
     engineering ledger documenting project breakthroughs.

3) Unified Terminology & Path Consistency:
   - Standardize terminology across all documents:
     * "GPLv3 Platinum Shield" (🛡️)
     * "Psychic Pre-Flight & Symlink Anchor" (`runepkg_psychic.c`)
     * "Antimatter Decontamination Chamber & Boundary Guard" (`runepkg_antimatter.c`)
     * "Zero-Day Clean-Room Utility Suite & C Library" (`arch-utils` / `libarchutils.so`)
     * "Optional Deployment & Conflict Prevention" (`/opt/arch-utils/bin/`)
     * "Stripped Environment Execution" (`PATH=/nonexistent`)

4) Standardized GPLv3 Comment Headers:
   - Ensure source code headers and key text artifacts strictly conform to
     `comment-header-master.txt`.
