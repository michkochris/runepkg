--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 0. THE GOLDEN RULE FOR BEGINNERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Only gem, enchant, and socket your PERMANENT Conquest or Crafted PvP gear!
 Save your gold while wearing starter Honor gear—you will replace it quickly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. STAT PRIORITY (PVP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Intellect    : Primary stat — increases all healing and damage output.
 2. Versatility  : Target 30%+ — boosts healing/damage AND reduces damage taken.
 3. Mastery      : Mastery: Lightbringer — increases healing based on proximity.
 4. Haste        : Faster cast times and shorter global cooldowns (GCD).
 5. Crit         : Lowest priority due to the PvP critical strike damage nerf.

 Core Tip: Focus on Versatility until surviving enemy burst feels comfortable,
 then pour remaining stats into Mastery for maximum healing throughput.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. PVP GEMS & SOCKET SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Sockets do not drop naturally on PvP gear; you add them manually.

 Adding Sockets:
   - Buy Jeweler's Settings from the PvP Vendor using Honor, Bloody Tokens,
     or Conquest.
   - Socketable slots: Helm, Neck, Bracers, Belt, and Rings.
   - Crafted Necks and Rings AUTOMATICALLY include sockets!

 How to Insert Gems:
   - Hold SHIFT + Right-Click gear in your character pane to open socket window.
   - Drag gem from inventory into socket slot and click Apply.

 Meta Gem (Limit: 1 UNIQUE per entire gear set):
   * Cognitive Heliotrope  : Grants Precognition (Interrupt/CC immunity if enemy
                             kicks you while you aren't casting).
   * Enduring Heliotrope   : Passive damage reduction while stunned or CC'd.

 Standard Gems (Fill ALL remaining sockets):
   * Flawless Masterful Lapis    : +Mastery (For higher healing throughput)
   * Flawless Versatile Amethyst : +Versatility (For extra survivability)

 Socket Priority Order:
   1. Socket #1 (Neck or Ring) -> Insert Meta Gem immediately.
   2. Jewelry (Neck & both Rings) -> Up to 2 sockets per piece.
   3. Armor Slots (Helm, Bracers, Belt) -> 1 socket per piece.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. CRAFTED PVP GEAR & EMBELLISHMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 You can equip up to TWO Embellishments (special passive armor procs) at once.
 Crafted PvP gear scales up to maximum Conquest item level in Arenas/BGs.

 Top Embellishments:
   - 2x Blessed Pango Charm  : Procs extra Versatility for you and allies.
   - 2x Arcanoweave Lining   : Increases Intellect/Primary stat for team.

 Recommended Slots to Craft (Thalassian Competitor's items):
   - Neck, Rings, Cloak, or Boots.
   - (Neck and Rings automatically include sockets!).
   - Avoid crafting in Tier slots (Helm, Chest, Gloves, Legs).

 How to Place a Crafting Order (Silvermoon City):
   1. Step 1 (Mats)    : Go to Crafting Order NPC in Silvermoon, find item,
                         click "Track Recipe", buy materials from AH.
   2. Step 2 (Reagents): Buy 2x Missive of the Harmonious (Mastery + Vers)
                         and your 2x Embellishments on AH.
   3. Step 3 (Submit)  : Add Gladiator's Heraldry (from win quest or Conquest
                         vendor), Missive, and Embellishment. Add a gold tip
                         and submit a Public Order.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. 4-PIECE TIER SET & MATRIX CATALYST GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Get 4 pieces out of 5 available slots. Skip Shoulders!

 Recommended Tier Slots (Buy Conquest pieces -> Convert at Catalyst):
   - Helm   : Venomous Gladiator's Plate Helm       -> Warhelm of Consecrated Flame
   - Chest  : Venomous Gladiator's Chestplate       -> Bulwark of Consecrated Flame
   - Gloves : Venomous Gladiator's Plate Handguards -> Gauntlets of Consecrated Flame
   - Legs   : Venomous Gladiator's Plate Tasses     -> Greaves of Consecrated Flame
   - Shoulders: Keep non-tier with Mastery/Vers (e.g. Crafted Pauldrons).

 How to Convert at Matrix Catalyst:
   - Location: Silvermoon City (/way 40.6 64.6 or /way 70.0 83.3).
   - Console: Right-click console, place Conquest piece, click Transform.
   - Uses 1x Dawnlight Manaflux charge (keeps full PvP iLvl scaling).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 6. COMPLETE CONQUEST GEAR PURCHASING ROADMAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Order of spending Conquest points:
   1. Weapon & Shield:
      * Venomous Gladiator's Cudgel (1H Mace) + Spikeshield.
      * TIP: Earning 2,500 total Conquest in a season gives a free Weapon token!
   2. PvP Trinket 2-Piece Bonus:
      * Venomous Gladiator's Medallion (Stun Break) + Insignia of Alacrity.
      * Equipping 2 PvP trinkets gives a huge hidden stat + Stamina bonus.
   3. 4-Piece Tier Slots:
      * Helm, Chest, Gloves, Legs -> Convert at Catalyst.
   4. Remaining Off-Pieces:
      * Wristguards (Bracers) and Girdle (Belt).
   5. Crafted Slots:
      * Neck, Rings, Cloak, Boots, Shoulders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 7. HERO TALENTS & PVP TALENTS (LIGHTSMITH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Hero Talent Tree: Lightsmith
   - Synergizes with 4-piece Tier Set, triggers extra Infusion of Light procs,
     and reduces Lay on Hands cooldown.
   - Gameplay Loop: Keep Beacon active -> Generate 3 Holy Power with Holy Shock
     -> Spend on Word of Glory -> Weave Infusion procs (Flash of Light /
     Greater Judgment).

 Recommended PvP Talents (Pick 3):
   1. Divine Vision           : MANDATORY (+30yd Aura range, -60s Aura Mastery CD)
   2. Blessing of Spellwarding: MANDATORY vs Casters (Spell immunity)
   3. Searing Glare / Cleanse :
      - Searing Glare vs Melee/Caster burst windows (causes misses).
      - Cleanse the Weak vs Heavy magic/poison dispel teams (Aff Warlock/SPriest).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 8. FAST SOLO CONQUEST FARMING METHODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. War Mode Supply Crates (Zero Gear Needed / Easiest):
    - Turn on War Mode in Silvermoon.
    - Premade Groups -> Custom -> Search "Crate".
    - Join shard-hopping raid group. Each crate = 60 Conquest, 150 Honor,
      50 Bloody Tokens.

 2. Solo Shuffle (Fastest Healer Queues):
    - Instant queues for healers. 3/6 wins awards Conquest + bonus supply boxes.

 3. Daily First Wins & Weekly Quests:
    - Grab weeklies from Zerella in Silvermoon.
    - Win 1x Random BG, 1x Epic BG, and 1x Arena Skirmish daily for first win bonus.
    - Win 1 non-training BG for free 9x Gladiator's Heraldries quest item.
